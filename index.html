A few helpful answers

Before we get going.

Not sure what you need yet? That’s a perfectly good place to start.

How does a project usually work?

We start with a short discovery call, agree on goals and scope, then move through structure, design, development, and launch with clear check-ins along the way.

How long will my website take?

A focused landing page often takes two to three weeks. A larger business website typically takes four to eight weeks, depending on content and feedback.

Can you work with clients internationally?

Absolutely. Projects are handled remotely with clear milestones, shared notes, and meeting times agreed across time zones.

Will I be able to update the website?

Yes. The handover is tailored to the build, with a walkthrough and practical notes so you know how to manage the content you own.
